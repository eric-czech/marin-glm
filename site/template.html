Tasks are organized by application, organism, model capability and functional region. A task is a dataset × prediction target; assay conditions and protocols are linked variants.

Organism filters include descendant taxa. Assignments distinguish sequence origin from assay host where established. Application mappings are editorial, not validation claims. Missing annotations do not imply biological absence.
